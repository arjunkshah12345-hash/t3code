# Tung Tung Tung Code launch film: SPEC (storyline A, "3 A.M.")

1920×1080, 30 fps, 37 s. Music + product sound effects, no voiceover.
Spine: the real prompt "Add a snooze to the sahur alarm", from the stalled approval to a merged PR.

## Look (numbers)
- Sky: night `#06050c → #2e1b1d`; dawn `#2a2340 → #f7c682`. The crossfade runs 14.2–29.2 s, sine.inOut.
- Type: DM Sans 600, tracking −0.025 to −0.045 em. Clock 280 px; titles 156–168 px; captions 46–66 px; UI text 26–38 px (the app at about 1.8× its size). JetBrains Mono for code and the URL.
- Accent: amber `#eba854` / `#f4b05c`. Status colours come from the app: Working sky-300/80, Pending Approval amber-300/90, Completed emerald-300/90.
- Material: one village skyline silhouette (back and front layers, lit windows).
- Banned: dark void, neon glow, three cards, a docked logo, tilted UI, fake app chrome.
