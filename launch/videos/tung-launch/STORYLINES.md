# Tung Tung Tung Code launch film: storylines

The one thing: your agents keep working through the night, and a wooden knock wakes you only when one needs you.
Spine: one real prompt from the product shots, "Add a snooze to the sahur alarm", travels from prompt to merged PR.
Assumptions: 30–40 s, 1920×1080 at 30 fps, music plus wooden-knock sound effects, no voiceover, free music. Real screenshots only (apps/marketing/src/assets/shots), shown at 1.6–2× size, never tilted.

## A. 3 A.M. (recommended: a situation everyone who runs agents knows)
- Device and form: a moment in someone's night, in one continuous take. The sky behind the app moves from deep night to dawn over the whole film, and a clock ticks from 02:58 to 06:00.
- What you see: one prompt fans out into five agent threads running side by side. Then one stalls on "needs approval" while the screen dims and nobody notices. TUNG. TUNG. TUNG. The frame jolts awake, the approval goes through, the diff reads clean and the PR merges as the sun clears the horizon.
- First two seconds: a big 02:58 clock over a dark village skyline, with five thread rows already ticking in the app beneath it.
- Beats: hook (02:58, agents running) → tension (one thread blocked, silence, the clock jumps) → reveal (three knocks, title "Wake every agent") → proof (approve, read diff, commit + PR) → payoff (PR merged as the sun rises) → claim ("Ship before dawn") → end card (the mascot carries the wordmark in, then the install command).
- Look: the site's night-to-dawn amber sky, DM Sans 600 titles, wood-grain texture as the one material.
- Sound: low night ambience and a slow pad. The three knocks are the loudest hits in the film, and the music turns warm after them.

## B. Knock, knock, knock
- Device and form: type carries the argument, cut on the beat. The three-knock rhythm is the drum track, and every knock lands a hard cut.
- What you see: giant words alternating with tight punch-ins on real UI, a new verb each beat (pick, run, read, ship). It speeds up, then stops dead on a single TUNG.
- First two seconds: black wood texture, then KNOCK: the word "tung" slams in at full frame width.
- Beats: hook (tung) → tension (agents piling up, counters climbing) → reveal (TUNG TUNG TUNG Code) → proof (four verbs on four beats) → payoff (all six agent logos lock into one composer) → claim → end card.
- Look: amber on near-black, huge tight-tracked type, hard cuts with no fades.
- Sound: a percussive track built from the wooden knocks themselves.

## C. The guardian's rounds (the bold one)
- Device and form: a character does the work, as one endless zoom. The log mascot (the real art) walks the village with his bat, and every pane he knocks on is a door the camera dives through.
- What you see: he passes sleeping threads, bats one awake, the camera zooms into its diff, out through the PR button, into the next pane, and finally into his eye for the end card.
- First two seconds: the mascot in silhouette against the dawn sky raises the bat, and the first knock lands on frame 30.
- Beats: hook (guardian on patrol) → tension (a thread asleep on approval) → reveal (bat knock, title) → proof (zoom through composer, diff, PR) → payoff (the whole village lit up) → claim → end card.
- Look: the guardian art cut out and lit in amber, with the village sky as the home stage.
- Sound: footsteps, wooden knocks and a playful pizzicato theme.
