## Design Context

### Users

Developers running their own coding-agent subscriptions, who want a usable workspace with a committed Sahur identity rather than a superficial logo swap.

### Brand Personality

Playful, wooden, nocturnal. The software stays dependable; the brand can be ridiculous.

### Aesthetic Direction

Warm timber, cream paper, amber accents, oversized editorial typography, and an original wooden guardian. The marketing site should feel like a village field guide, not a generic AI startup. Keep local fonts and vector art; no remote meme dependencies.

### Design Principles

- Preserve real coding workflows and compatible integration identifiers.
- Make secrets deliberate, discoverable, resettable, and silent by default.
- Keep editors, terminals, IME input, and shortcuts out of easter-egg handlers.
- No continuous animation or surprise audio; respect reduced motion.
- Preserve upstream license attribution without impersonating its hosted service or releases.
